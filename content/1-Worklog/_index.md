# Internship Worklog (8-Week Journal)

Throughout the **8-week internship** under the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ 2026)** program at **AWS Vietnam** (from **June 15, 2026** to **July 31, 2026**), I conducted research, cloud architecture design, and production deployment of **LifeSync AI Calendar** — a scientific AI-powered smart scheduling web platform.

Below is the weekly summary of tasks and milestones:

---

### 📅 8-Week Internship Outline:

- **[Week 1 (June 15 – June 21): On-boarding & AWS Service Fundamentals](1.1-Week1/)**  
  *On-site at AWS Office (June 15)*. Received AWS Credits, configured development environment, and studied core cloud services (IAM, EC2, S3, VPC, Lambda, RDS).

- **[Week 2 (June 22 – June 28): Ideation & MySQL Database Schema Design](1.2-Week2/)**  
  *On-site at AWS Office (June 24)*. Project ideation with Tien Kha, tested LocalStack, and designed relational database schemas on MySQL.

- **[Week 3 (June 29 – July 05): Swinburne Cloud Mastery Seminar & Haversine Proximity Algorithm](1.3-Week3/)**  
  *On-site at AWS Office (July 04)*. Attended Swinburne Cloud Mastery seminar, developed Haversine proximity algorithm with "Nam rước Nữ" weighting (80% Female / 20% Male), and integrated NextAuth.js Google OAuth 2.0.

- **[Week 4 (July 06 – July 12): Provisioning EC2, Elastic IP, Nginx & Strategy Pattern Scheduler](1.4-Week4/)**  
  *On-site at AWS Office (July 08 & July 11)*. Launched Ubuntu EC2 instance, attached Elastic IP `3.104.121.77`, configured Nginx Reverse Proxy, PM2, developed scheduling strategies (`StudyStrategy`, `FitnessStrategy`, `DateStrategy`), and presented Mid-term report.

- **[Week 5 (July 13 – July 19): AWS Account Incident & Deploying RDS MySQL + S3 Presigned URLs](1.5-Week5/)**  
  *Incident on July 15*: Resolved primary AWS account organization lock ➔ Migrated database & codebase to secondary AWS account. Configured RDS MySQL and S3 avatar upload via Presigned URLs.

- **[Week 6 (July 20 – July 26): AABW Hackathon Workshop & Deploying AWS WAF + CloudFront](1.6-Week6/)**  
  *On-site at AWS Office (July 25)*. Attended AABW AWS AI Build Week workshop, deployed CloudFront CDN, and configured AWS WAF (Core Rules + SQL Injection protection).

- **[Week 7 (July 27 – July 29): Dual-AI Engine Integration (Bedrock + Gemini), Lambda CGV & Security Audit](1.7-Week7/)**  
  Integrated **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** as a high-availability fallback AI engine for Bedrock, deployed AWS Lambda + EventBridge for weekly CGV cinema scraping, configured AWS Cloud9/CloudShell, and performed security audit (WAF, RDS, S3 policies).

- **[Week 8 (July 30 – July 31): Nginx Performance Optimization, 4 Technical Blogs & Final Submission](1.8-Week8/)**  
  Optimized Nginx Gzip/caching & load testing (`ab`), authored 4 technical blog posts (Bedrock vs SageMaker, DevOps Agent, CloudWatch Alarms, Data Protection), completed self-evaluation & FCAJ feedback, and **officially submitted the final internship report (July 31, 2026)**.
