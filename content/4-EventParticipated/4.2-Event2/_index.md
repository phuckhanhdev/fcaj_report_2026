# Summary Report: “GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization Workshop”

### Event Objectives

- Share best practices in modern application design and cloud modernization
- Introduce Domain-Driven Design (DDD) and event-driven architecture
- Provide guidance on selecting the right compute services (EC2 vs Fargate vs Lambda)
- Present AI tools like Amazon Q Developer to support the development lifecycle

### Event Details

- **Date & Time:** June 27, 2026
- **Location:** 36th Floor, AWS Vietnam Office, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

### Speakers

- **Jignesh Shah** – Director, Open Source Databases, AWS
- **Erica Liu** – Sr. GTM Specialist, AppMod
- **Fabrianne Effendi** – Associate Specialist SA, Serverless AWS

---

### Key Highlights

#### 1. Transitioning to Microservices & Event-Driven Architecture
- Migrating monolithic systems to independent microservices communicating via asynchronous events.
- Core pillars: Queue management, Caching strategies, and Messaging patterns (Pub/Sub, Point-to-point, Streaming).

#### 2. Domain-Driven Design (DDD)
- Applying DDD principles to identify domain events, bounded contexts, and ubiquitous language across tech and business teams.

#### 3. Compute Evolution & Amazon Q Developer
- Exploring the compute spectrum: EC2 → ECS → Fargate → Lambda Serverless.
- Leveraging Amazon Q Developer for automated code transformation, SDLC acceleration, and database modernization.

---

### Key Takeaways

- **Business-First Approach**: Always start application modernization from business domains rather than technology trends.
- **Serverless & Event-Driven**: Reduce operational overhead and maximize scalability by adopting serverless patterns.

> Overall, the workshop provided actionable frameworks for modernizing applications, databases, and development workflows on AWS.
