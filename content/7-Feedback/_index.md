### Overall Assessment

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam** is extremely open, dynamic, and professional. Mentors and members of the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program were always enthusiastic and ready to assist whenever I encountered technical roadblocks, even outside regular office hours. The office space on the 36th floor provided a comfortable environment that helped maximize my focus.

**2. Support from Mentor & Admin Team**  
My mentor provided exceptionally dedicated guidance, explaining underlying cloud concepts thoroughly while encouraging an analytical approach to troubleshooting. The admin team assisted smoothly with access rights, event logistics, and documentation, allowing interns to focus fully on engineering.

**3. Alignment between Work and Academic Major**  
The assigned project tasks aligned directly with my Software Engineering & Cloud computing studies at university, while introducing practical enterprise dimensions (such as AWS WAF hardening, Nginx reverse proxy tuning, and S3 Presigned URLs).

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
Throughout the internship, I gained hands-on experience across core AWS services (EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront, WAF, Lambda, EventBridge), AI models (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Amazon Bedrock), CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions), and system architecture diagramming.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company fosters a strong culture of mutual respect, transparency, and continuous learning. Team spirit was evident during hackathons and workshop sessions, making me feel like a valued contributor to the team.

**6. Policies & Benefits for Interns**  
Intern support policies were highly satisfactory, including access to AWS Learning platforms, AWS Credits for hands-on labs, and privileges to attend major tech seminars and hackathons.

### Additional Questions

- **What are you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
  Being exposed to real-world cloud deployment workflows, working directly with AWS experts on the 36th floor office, and shipping a live production web application ([https://phuckhanh.id.vn](https://phuckhanh.id.vn)) serving real users.
- **What do you think the program could improve for future interns?**  
  I hope the program provides even more frequent on-site office work sessions, as learning directly alongside AWS engineers at the office accelerates skill growth significantly.
- **Would you recommend this internship to friends? Why?**  
  Absolutely yes. FCAJ is one of the most practical and inspiring cloud internship programs for students to bridge the gap between university theory and enterprise cloud architecture.

### Proposals & Desires

  Providing early orientation on AWS Credit management and account boundary configurations to help new interns avoid accidental account organizational locks.
- **Do you want to continue accompanying this program in the future?**  
  I sincerely hope to continue collaborating with the FCAJ community and aspire to contribute as a Cloud / DevOps Engineer in the future.
- **Expressing Gratitude:**  
  I would like to express my deepest gratitude to the AWS Vietnam team, FCAJ program admins, and especially my mentor for their constant guidance, patience, and support throughout this journey.
